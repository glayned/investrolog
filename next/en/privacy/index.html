Privacy policy

Who processes the data

Dmitry Maltsev, author of InvestroLog × DataMatrix.
Contact: dmitry.maltcev@investrolog.pro.

What data

Only the email address you enter in the “Matrix by email” form, the page language, and the dates of your request, confirmation and unsubscription. No name, phone or payment data is requested. Your network address is not stored: to protect the form from automated requests only its irreversible fingerprint is kept, for no longer than a day.

Why

To send you emails with the commodity matrix and notices about the project. The address is not used for other advertising and is not shared with third parties.

Legal basis

Your consent: you enter the address in the form and confirm it by the link in the email. Without confirmation no emails are sent.

Where it is stored and who sends the emails

Addresses are kept in private storage in Yandex Cloud on servers in Russia. Emails are sent through Yandex Cloud Postbox. There are no addresses on the site or in the project's public code.

How long

While the subscription is active. An unconfirmed address is deleted after 30 days. After you unsubscribe, emails stop at once; the record is deleted on request.

How to unsubscribe and delete data

Every email has an “Unsubscribe” link. To delete the address completely or learn what data is stored about you, write to matrix@investrolog.pro — we reply within 10 working days.

Cookies

The subscription form does not use cookies.
